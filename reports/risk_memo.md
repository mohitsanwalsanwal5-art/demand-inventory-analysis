```
                 │   • Stock <= Reorder Point │   • Low physical on-hand stock

                 │   HEALTHY (17 SKUs)        │   MARKDOWN / CLEAR (18 SKUs)

                 │   • Stock <= 4-week demand │   • Holding excess inventory
                 │   • Action: Maintain       │   • Action: Clear / Discount
           (0.0) └────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────►
               (0.0)                        (0.5)                       (1.0)

```

### A. Lead-Time Demand Formulation
Lead times across NorthBay suppliers range from **3 to 14 days** (mean: 7.84 days). To project expected demand during the replenishment window without data leakage:
* Let $\hat{y}_{\text{week 1}}$ and $\hat{y}_{\text{week 2}}$ be the forward forecasts for the upcoming two weeks.
* If $\text{Lead\_Time\_Days} \le 7$:
  $$\text{lead\_time\_demand} = \left(\frac{\hat{y}_{\text{week 1}}}{7}\right) \times \text{Lead\_Time\_Days}$$
* If $\text{Lead\_Time\_Days} > 7$ (e.g. 8 to 14 days):
  $$\text{lead\_time\_demand} = \hat{y}_{\text{week 1}} + \left(\frac{\hat{y}_{\text{week 2}}}{7}\right) \times (\text{Lead\_Time\_Days} - 7)$$

### B. Available Inventory & Stockout Gap
* **Available Stock:**
  $$\text{available\_units} = \text{Current\_Stock} + \text{On\_Order}$$
* **Required Buffer:**
  $$\text{required\_buffer} = \text{lead\_time\_demand} + \text{Safety\_Stock}$$
* **Reorder Gap (Physical On-Hand Deficit):**
  $$\text{reorder\_gap\_units} = \max\left(0, \text{Reorder\_Point} - \text{Current\_Stock}\right)$$
* **Buffer Gap (Net Pipeline Deficit):**
  $$\text{buffer\_gap\_units} = \max\left(0, \text{required\_buffer} - \text{available\_units}\right)$$

### C. Continuous Risk Scoring Rules $[0.0, 1.0]$
* **Stockout Risk Score:**
  $$\text{stockout\_score} = \text{clip}\left(1.0 - 0.5 \times \frac{\text{Current\_Stock}}{\text{Reorder\_Point}}, 0.0, 1.0\right)$$
  * *Threshold Behavior:* $\text{Current\_Stock} \le \text{Reorder\_Point} \iff \text{stockout\_score} \ge 0.50$ (`stockout_flag = 1`).
* **Overstock Risk Score:**
  Evaluated against forward 4-week forecast demand ($\text{forward\_demand\_units} = \sum_{h=1}^4 \hat{y}_h$):
  $$\text{overstock\_score} = \text{clip}\left(0.5 \times \frac{\text{Current\_Stock}}{\text{forward\_demand\_units}}, 0.0, 1.0\right)$$
  * *Threshold Behavior:* $\text{Current\_Stock} > \text{forward\_demand\_units} \iff \text{overstock\_score} \ge 0.50$ (`overstock_flag = 1`).
  * $\text{excess\_units} = \max\left(0, \text{Current\_Stock} - \text{forward\_demand\_units}\right)$.

### D. Financial Rupee Exposure Formulas
* **Revenue at Risk (₹):**
  $$\text{revenue\_at\_risk} = \text{units\_at\_stockout\_risk} \times \text{Selling\_Price}$$
  *(Where $\text{units\_at\_stockout\_risk} = \text{reorder\_gap\_units}$ for SKUs with $\text{stockout\_flag} = 1$)*
* **Inventory Value Tied Up (₹):**
  $$\text{inventory\_value\_tied\_up} = \text{excess\_units} \times \text{Cost\_Price}$$
* **Total Value at Stake (₹):**
  $$\text{value\_at\_stake} = \text{revenue\_at\_risk} + \text{inventory\_value\_tied\_up}$$

## 5. Assumptions, Limitations & Governance

1. **Non-Transactional Nature:** The risk engine produces **decision-support recommendations only**. It does not automatically generate or transmit purchase orders to external vendors.
2. **Static Lead Times:** Lead times are based on the client extract values (`Lead_Time_Days`) and assume on-time delivery from suppliers without unforeseen transit delays.
3. **Holding Target Rationale:** The 4-week forward demand threshold for overstocking represents 1 month of customer supply. In D2C furniture and lifestyle goods, holding $>4$ weeks of inventory increases warehouse storage costs and capital depreciation.
4. **Terminology Transparency:** Values are classified as **"Revenue at Risk"** (estimated unmet demand $\times$ selling price) and **"Inventory Value Tied Up"** (excess units $\times$ cost price), rather than guaranteed accounting losses.
